The S.I.F.T. Verification Protocol

A proven four-step framework developed by digital literacy researchers at the University of Washington.

S

1. Stop

When an article provokes intense anger, panic, or vindication, pause before sharing. High emotional resonance is the primary mechanism of viral disinformation.

I

2. Investigate Source

Who published this? Check the domain URL for typosquatting (e.g. '.com.co'). Research the author's credentials outside of their self-published bio.

F

3. Find Trusted Coverage

Are credible global wire services (AP, Reuters, BBC) reporting this claim? If a catastrophic event occurred, multiple independent outlets will confirm it.

T

4. Trace Context

Trace quotes, charts, and video clips to their unedited source. Often, genuine footage is repackaged with fabricated misleading captions years later.

Common Linguistic Hallmarks of Misinformation

Hyperbolic & Urgent Framing

Phrases like "Shocking Bombshell", "They don't want you to know", or "Share before it is deleted" artificially manufacture panic to hijack rationality.

Anonymous & Vague Authorities

Claims citing "Scientists are stunned" or "Top doctors reveal" without naming researchers, universities, or peer-reviewed citations.

Miracle & Panacea Narratives

No single kitchen ingredient or herb cures every complex illness overnight. Health misinformation frequently monetizes false hope for dietary supplements.

Lack of Primary Data

Real journalism links directly to official court filings, academic papers, legislative bills, or photographic corroboration.
